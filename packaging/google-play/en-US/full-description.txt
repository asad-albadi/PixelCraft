Make pixel art wherever you like to draw.

PixelCraft is a free, open-source pixel art and animation editor for Android tablets and phones. Sketch a sprite, organize its layers, and bring it to life frame by frame. Drawing and editing work locally, without a PixelCraft account, ads, or in-app purchases.

DRAW AND REFINE
Use the pencil, eraser, fill, eyedropper, lines, rectangles and ellipses. Choose brush sizes and shapes, keep named colors close at hand, and zoom in to work on individual pixels. Undo and Redo let you explore without losing your place.

KEEP YOUR ARTWORK EDITABLE
Build with layers and groups. Adjust visibility, opacity and order, and work with linked cels. Select, copy, move, flip, rotate or crop your artwork. Native .pixel projects keep supported editing information so you can continue on desktop or Android, within the Android project limits.

ANIMATE FRAME BY FRAME
Add and duplicate frames, set their durations, name animation ranges, and choose playback direction and speed. Use onion skin to compare neighboring frames, and watch your animation in the Preview panel.

MADE FOR TABLETS, ADAPTED FOR PHONES
Keep your palette and Preview beside the canvas on a tablet, or open compact sheets on a phone. Switch between light and dark themes. Zen drawing mode hides most controls while keeping essential tools within reach. Touch, keyboard, mouse and supported stylus input are available, with optional pressure-sensitive brush size.

OPEN, SAVE AND EXPORT
Save editable PixelCraft projects, open supported PNG/APNG, GIF and Aseprite files, and export PNG images, animated GIFs, supported ASE files, or a PNG sprite sheet with matching JSON metadata. Use Android's file picker and sharing controls to choose where files go. Format support varies; not every feature from another editor is preserved.

TILES AND REFERENCES
Create a tileset from your artwork, stamp tiles, and add embedded image references as drawing guides.

CURRENT ANDROID LIMITS
This early Android version supports canvases up to 256 x 256 pixels, 32 layers and 64 frames, with file and memory limits. It does not yet include every desktop feature. Keep backups of important work, especially before changing between test and release builds.
