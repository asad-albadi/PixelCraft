# PixelCraft privacy policy

Draft prepared November 23, 2026. Review against the final signed Android build before publication.

PixelCraft is a free, open-source pixel art and animation editor published by AstralHive. This policy describes how the Android app handles information.

## Artwork and local storage

PixelCraft processes your drawings, imported images, palettes, animation frames and project information on your device. It stores private saves and recovery data in the app's local storage. PixelCraft does not operate an account service or upload your artwork to a PixelCraft server.

You can delete private app data through Android Settings or by uninstalling the app. Exported files remain in the locations you chose until you delete them there. Android system backup or device-transfer services may handle app data according to your device and account settings.

## Opening, exporting and sharing files

PixelCraft uses Android's document picker to access files you select. If you select an export folder, the app can retain Android's permission to use that folder; forgetting the folder releases that access.

When you explicitly share an export, Android gives the receiving app temporary read access to the selected exported file. The receiving app or document provider may be an online service and handles that file under its own terms and privacy policy. PixelCraft does not control that service.

## Analytics, advertising and accounts

The Android app does not include advertising, an analytics service, a PixelCraft account system or a built-in crash-reporting service. It does not request your contacts, location, camera or microphone for its editing features. Google Play, Android and device vendors may separately provide diagnostics or store services under their own policies.

## External links

The Privacy policy menu entry opens this policy on GitHub in your browser. The browser and GitHub handle that page request under their own settings and privacy policies. Reading the online policy requires an internet connection; PixelCraft does not download or bundle the policy itself.

## Support

If you email support or submit a public GitHub issue, the information you choose to provide is handled to respond to your request. GitHub issues are public; avoid including private artwork, passwords or personal information you do not want to publish. Email and GitHub are separate services with their own policies.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if PixelCraft's data handling changes. Questions or deletion requests concerning information you sent to support can be directed to **asad.badi@astralhive.om**.
