PixelCraft comes to Android phones and tablets.

- Draw with pixel tools, named colors and selections.
- Organize layers and animate with frames, tags and onion skin.
- Save editable projects and export images, GIFs and sprite sheets.
- Focus on your artwork in Zen drawing mode.
- Use touch or a supported stylus, with optional pressure-sensitive brush size.

This early Android version supports up to 256 x 256 pixels, 32 layers and 64 frames.
